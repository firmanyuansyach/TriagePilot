# Terms of Service — TriagePilot

Effective date: 26 November 2026

TriagePilot is a free Forge app for Jira, built by Firman Yuansyach for the Codegeist 2026 hackathon.

## Use

You may install and use TriagePilot on your Atlassian site. The app proposes triage suggestions; every suggestion must be reviewed by a human before approval. Approving a proposal changes the issue's priority, adds a label, and posts a comment, exactly as shown in the panel.
